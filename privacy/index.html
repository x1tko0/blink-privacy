Blink

Eye Care, Screen Timer — Privacy Policy

Short version

Everything stays on your phone. Blink has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting SDKs. The app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere.

Data the app stores on your device

  • Your breaks and eye exercises (time, length, which exercise).
  • Daily screen time summaries (minutes per hour, number of sessions, longest stretch) — only if you grant screen time access.
  • Your settings (interval, quiet hours, sounds, theme) and purchase status.

This data is stored in the app's private storage. It is never uploaded by Blink.

Screen time access (Usage Access)

Granting it is optional. If you allow it in Android settings, Blink reads only the system events "screen turned on" and "screen turned off". It uses them to avoid reminding you while the screen is off and to show how long the screen was on.

Blink does not read which apps you use, your app history, messages, browsing or any other content. You can revoke the access at any time in Android settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications — break reminders, the end-of-break signal, optional daily summary.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) — optional, for reminders right on time.
  • Usage access — optional, see above.
  • Run at startup — restart the break timer after the phone reboots.
  • Vibration — silent signals and guidance for eyes-closed exercises.
  • Google Play Billing — the one-time Pro purchase.

Purchases

The one-time Pro purchase is processed by Google Play. Blink never sees your payment details; Google's privacy policy applies to the payment.

Backups and deleting data

You can export a backup file yourself (Settings → Data) and choose where to save it. Android's own device backup may include app data if you enabled it in your phone settings. To delete everything, use Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the app.

Not a medical app

Blink helps you take regular eye breaks and do relaxation exercises. It does not diagnose, treat or correct any condition. If you have eye pain or vision problems, see an eye doctor.

Children

Blink is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
